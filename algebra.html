What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent unknown values.

Example

Consider the equation:

x + 5 = 12

We want to find the value of x.

Subtract 5 from both sides:

x = 12 - 5

Therefore:

x = 7
